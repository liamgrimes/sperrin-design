Who we are

Sperrin Design operates this website. For questions about this notice or your personal information, please contact us through our contact page.

We use enquiry information to respond to you and, where relevant, take steps you request before a purchase or fulfil an agreement. We may also retain records where needed for legitimate business purposes or legal obligations. We do not use contact-form details for marketing unless you separately ask or consent to receive it.

Service providers and international transfers

We use service providers to deliver website functions, email, spam prevention and checkout. The contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to distinguish people from automated submissions. When it loads or is used, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser information and interaction with the challenge. Shopify powers the store cart and checkout. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices, and may process it outside your country.
