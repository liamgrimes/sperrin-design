About cookies

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Do we track cookies?

We do not currently use our own analytics or advertising cookies or run our own cross-site tracking. The site does load third-party services that may use cookies or similar browser storage as described below.

Third-party services

  • Shopify: The storefront's Buy Button and checkout are provided by Shopify. Shopify may use cookies or browser storage to support cart and checkout functions, security and service operation.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: The contact form uses reCAPTCHA for spam prevention. Google may set or read cookies and process technical and interaction information to assess whether a submission is automated.

These services are operated by their providers and are subject to their own privacy and cookie information. See our Privacy policy, Google's Privacy Policy and Shopify's Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies

You can use your browser settings to view, block or delete cookies. Blocking third-party cookies or storage may affect the contact form, shopping cart or checkout. Browser controls do not necessarily stop all data processing by third-party services.

Changes

If the site's use of cookies or similar technologies changes, we will update this page.
